# Soulseek: your responsibility

Soulseek is a network run by its users. What you download or share there is your responsibility. Share only what you have the right to share and download only what you are allowed to have.

Aureon does not connect to Soulseek, search it, or share anything until you turn Soulseek on in Settings → General and sign in yourself. Sharing is a separate switch, off by default, and only the folders you tick are ever visible to others, under share names (never your folders or your user name).
