# Now Playing: Poster and Turntable (for the Windows native port)

Layout order: Stage, Classic, Full-bleed, Carousel, Chillax, Poster, Turntable. Lumen is Classic's wide-window Up next column (over 980 pt); Orbit is gone. A saved "lumen" opens Classic, "orbit" the default.

**Poster.** 40 pt margins and gutter (28 minimum when scaled down); the dock uses the same margin. Left column: label "Now playing · Track N of M" (album track numbers when tagged, else queue place); bold title, two lines at most, size = min(190, 2·0.9·width / (chars·0.54), height / 2.4), floor 28, tracking −3.5 %; artist · album · year; format tags; three lyric lines (previous dim, current bright, next dim; pauses skipped) or, with lyrics off or untimed, up to three credits. Right column about 300 pt: square cover, two credit rows (Produced by, then the next credit), hairline, Up next with one row. Backdrop: light theme, canvas mixed 60 % toward warm paper (0.965, 0.935, 0.875); dark theme, canvas mixed 40 % toward black.

**Turntable.** Fixed square (min of body height, 46 % of width, 640): plinth, platter, record (diameter 0.84 of the square, cover as a 36 % label, grooves with gaps between tracks, fixed highlights over the turning disc). One turn per 4 s while playing; holds its angle when paused; never turns with Reduce Motion. Render the disc once and rotate that layer. Tonearm: pivot (0.86, 0.12), length 0.70 of the side; the needle's distance from the record centre goes from 0.405 to 0.19 across the song (law of cosines gives the arm angle); at rest it swings 12° outward off the record. Right column: label, large title (max 120 pt), artist/album/year, tags, three lyric lines, hairline, Up next with two rows.
