# Aureon 0.5.0

This update opens Aureon to more Macs, adds a built-in Soulseek client, and gives Now Playing a new look.

**Works on macOS 13 Ventura and later**, on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. It used to need macOS 15.

## New

- **Soulseek.** A built-in client with search, downloads, uploads, browsing other users, chat rooms, private messages and a wishlist. It lives on its own page in the sidebar, and a Downloads button in the top bar shows what is moving. Downloads finish in a separate folder and join your library when complete, with the files left exactly as they came. It is off until you switch it on in Settings, and sharing stays off until you choose folders to share.
- **A clearer quality tag.** A Hi-Res, Lossless or Lossy chip sits on the cover. Under the artist, the format shows as a gold stamp, such as FLAC 24/96, followed by Exclusive, Signal path, Processing and Info. Each part opens its details in place. Hi-Res only appears for files that really are above CD quality.
- **Cover flow.** The current song sits in the middle with its neighbours on either side. Title, tags, lyrics and controls each have their own space and never overlap. It replaces the old Carousel.
- **Poster and Turntable.** Two new Now Playing layouts. On the Turntable you can drag the record to scratch, and the sound follows your hand.
- **Chillax** now shows as many queued songs as fit, up to fifteen.
- **New search.** Suggestions as you type, filters, a top result, recent searches, and a genre grid made from your own covers. It closes when you click away or press Esc.
- **DJ console.** Redesigned in the style of professional DJ software. It fits any window without scrolling, with the library in a drawer. DJ mode is optional and off until you turn it on.
- **New transport buttons.** Larger, solid and easier to hit, in the player bar, every layout and the mini player.
- **Extra panels.** Optional cards on Now Playing, such as plays, loudness and file details. They only show real data, at most two at a time, and each has its own switch in Settings → Now Playing.
- **Balanced Shuffle.** Shuffle that follows tempo, key and energy.

## Changed

- The minimum macOS is now 13.
- Top bar buttons have stronger outlines and darker icons on the light themes.

## Removed

- The Intelligence page and its settings, Smart DJ, natural-language playlists and semantic search. The Lumen and Orbit layouts are replaced by Poster and Turntable.

## Known issues

- Casting and multi-room streams can crash under heavy load.

## Found a bug, or want to help?

Please [open an issue](https://github.com/retrohog/Aureon/issues) with what happened, your Mac model and your macOS version. Pull requests are welcome too, and we will look at every one. [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) explains how to build and check your change.

Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 13 or later.
